# Spec: Content Guidelines (Satire Rules)

**Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-11-01

Every character, item, enemy, asset and line of text is checked against these rules **before** it goes into the game.

## 1. Punch up

- **The joke always lands on the speaker or the powerful, never on the group they're talking about.**
- **A character built on an insult is the answer to the quote, not its illustration.** The quote appears **only on the unlock card**, with source and context. The gameplay answers it.
- **No ethnic or religious visual markers or accents used as gags.** Characters are drawn through their job, situation or attitude.
- **The supposedly "lazy" archetypes** (Sozialtourist, Bürgergeld-Empfänger, Totalverweigerer…) are drawn as **likeable and competent**. The joke is that these builds win.
- **Exhaustion comes from circumstances, never weakness.** Characters are never pitiful or helpless, and nobody gets rescued by an outside hero.
- **Enemies and the powerful can be caricatured freely**, as long as that caricature doesn't break section 2.

## 2. Anti-capitalist satire without antisemitic codes

Taboo, in visuals, names and text:

- **Caricatures:** hooked noses, a small greedy man with money bags or an oversized briefcase.
- **Puppet imagery:** a puppet master pulling strings, an octopus with tentacles around the globe.
- **Vermin imagery:** rats, **"Heuschrecken"** (locusts) and other vermin metaphors.
- **Coded language:** "Globalisten", "Hochfinanz", Rothschild/Soros allusions.
- **Split capital:** the split between "raffendes" and "schaffendes" capital (only finance is evil, industry is good).
- **A shadowy lone string-puller who secretly controls everything.**

**Instead:**
- Critique **structures** (lobbying, rent, inheritance, privatization, Cum-Ex).
- Use **visibly German, recognizable types:** the Sylt Porsche driver, the Golfclub-Vorstand, the Aufsichtsrat in a quilted vest, the property heir with a Barbour jacket, the FDP-Jungunternehmer.

## 3. Real politicians

- **No real, identifiable politician is killed or physically harmed in the game.**
- **Bosses are parodies with invented names** (e.g. "Der Sauerländer"). When beaten, they're **"abgesetzt"** and sent **"ins politische Abseits"**, not killed.
- **No Nazi symbols** (§86a StGB), not even satirically, for example in AfD-related content.

## 4. Quotes

- **Guiding principle: everything that's legal goes.** "Said is said": a later apology or walk-back doesn't make a quote unusable, and it doesn't have to be mentioned.
- **Only verbatim quotes with a source** that are marked **`verifiziert`** in `research/zitate_de.json`.
  - `sinngemäß` quotes may not be presented as quotes.
  - `umstritten` (wording or attribution actually disputed) and `unverifiziert` quotes are not used.
- **Single-source quotes** need a highly reputable source (dpa, Tagesschau, ZDF…), or they get double-checked. If in doubt, leave them out.
- **Every quote shown anywhere in the game carries speaker, party, date and a short context line** (`kontext_kurz`, e.g. "über Adolf Hitler, im Gespräch mit Elon Musk"). The Album additionally shows the full context and sources.
- **The most drastic quotes are wanted**, especially NS and Holocaust relativization and racism. A racist quote about a group (even about children) is fine: shown with name and party, it always exposes the speaker. The punch-up rule (§1) is about *our* jokes and drawings, not about quoting what politicians actually said.
- **The start screen always shows one of the strongest quotes** (use `startbildschirm`).
- **No inventing, shortening or "smoothing"** a quote's wording. Satire is protected (Art. 5 GG), but a misquote is defamation risk (§§185–188 StGB, Persönlichkeitsrecht).
- **Our own opinions are allowed** (e.g. calling Höcke a fascist). We just never present an opinion as a court's finding or as a fact that a court ruled on.
- **Invented satire lines** must look clearly different from real quotes (no source, a different font).
- **Quotes on foreign policy or the Middle East** (e.g. Merz's "Drecksarbeit") are allowed. The target is always the speaker's worldview, never Jews or Israelis as a group (see §2).

## 5. Quality bar

- **Only really strong ideas go in.** A lean set beats many mediocre ones.
- **Mechanics must not contradict the visuals.** For example, enemies get more dangerous over a run, so they don't shrink.

## 6. Review before release

- Playtest with people from the affected groups (migrants, Bürgergeld recipients, Jewish players and others) to catch blind spots.
- Before launch, consider a legal check of the quotes and boss parodies.

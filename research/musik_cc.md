# Openly licensed ska / ska-punk music: research

**As of:** 2026-11-01 · **Rules:** [docs/specs/asset-licensing.md](../docs/specs/asset-licensing.md) · **No audio was downloaded.** Only links and page data were collected.

Every track below had its license **checked on its own page** (FMA track page, OGA entry, ccMixter file page, incompetech `pieces.json` / track page, Wikimedia Commons file page, or the Audionautix genre page). Tracks are grouped by use. Within each group they're sorted by fit.

**Fit rating:** 5 = exactly right, 1 = stopgap.
**Attribution line:** copy into `CREDITS.md` / the in-game credits. If a track is cut, looped or crossfaded, add "edited (cut/looped)".

> **General notes**
> - **FMA downloads** (`/track/<slug>/download/`) redirect to the login page. You need a free FMA account to download.
> - **ccMixter direct links** (`/content/...mp3`) returned 403 to a direct request (hotlink protection). Download from the track page in a browser.
> - **Take a screenshot of the license** when you download (spec §2). FMA and ccMixter pages change over time.
> - **Kevin MacLeod:** incompetech now states **CC BY 4.0**. Older copies (e.g. on Wikimedia Commons) still say 3.0. Use the 4.0 line. His tracks have ISRCs and are sometimes **falsely claimed by third parties in YouTube Content ID**. Not a licensing problem, but it can annoy streamers (claims can be disputed).
> - **FMA's "Instrumental: No" field** is often filled in inaccurately. Where no lyrics appear on the page, **listen first**.
